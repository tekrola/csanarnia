Have you ever imagined walking through an ordinary wardrobe and finding yourself in an entirely different world? In The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe by C.S. Lewis, four siblings—Peter, Susan, Edmund, and Lucy Pevensie—discover the enchanted land of Narnia, where animals talk, winter never ends, and every tree seems alive with mystery.

Guided by Aslan, the noble and powerful lion who symbolizes courage and truth, the children find themselves caught in an epic battle between good and evil against the White Witch, a tyrant who has cursed Narnia into a century of snow and despair. With the help of loyal Narnians like Mr. Tumnus, Mr. and Mrs. Beaver, and brave creatures of the land, they learn that even the smallest act of bravery can change a world frozen in fear.

This website is your gateway to explore the wonders of Narnia—from its characters and adventures to the lessons of faith, loyalty, and redemption that lie beneath its snowy surface. Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a returning traveler through the wardrobe, you’ll find exciting ways to relive the story and test your knowledge about this beloved classic.
